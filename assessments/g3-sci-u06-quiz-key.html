  1. What is a fossil?
    Multiple choice · 1 point · from lesson 17.01

    1. What is left of a living thing from long ago, like a bone, shell, leaf, or footprint ✓
    2. A rock that wind carved into the shape of an animal
    3. A plant or animal that is still alive today
    4. A crystal that grows deep underground

  2. A fish dies and sinks to the bottom of a lake. What has to happen next for it to become a fossil?
    Multiple choice · 1 point · from lesson 17.01

    1. Wind and rain wear the rock away.
    2. The fish floats back to the top.
    3. Mud covers it, layer after layer. ✓
    4. A scientist digs it up right away.

  3. A scientist finds a fossil coral in a dry desert. What does this evidence most likely tell us?
    Multiple choice · 1 point · from lesson 17.01

    1. The place has always been a dry desert.

    3. Coral lives in deserts today.
    4. The desert was once covered by a warm, shallow ocean. ✓
