🌱 Part 1Traits Help Plants Survive

Every plant has traits: features like leaf shape, root depth, or stem thickness. Some traits help a plant get water. Others help it catch sunlight, stay safe, or make seeds.

When a trait helps a plant survive in its environment, scientists call it an adaptation. A plant with a useful adaptation has an advantage over other plants.

🌵 Part 2Desert Plants vs. Rainforest Plants

Desert plants live with very little water. A cactus stores water in its thick, waxy stem. Its spines are really leaves with a new shape. They lose almost no water, and they keep animals away.

In a rainforest, rain falls almost every day. Many leaves are wide, to catch light in the shade. Some end in a pointed drip tip. Scientists think drip tips help leaves dry fast, so moss and mold don't grow on them. A trait that helps in one place may hurt in another.

🎬 Behind the scenes · A waxy coat
See how this drop sits on top of the leaf instead of soaking in? Many leaves have a thin, waxy coat. In a desert, wax helps hold water in. In the rain, it helps water roll off. Photo: a nasturtium leaf, a common garden plant.

🪴 Part 3Root Systems and Reproduction

The root system is an adaptation too. Desert cactuses have shallow roots that spread wide to catch rain fast. Many grassland plants have deep roots that live through dry weather and fire. Wetland plants have spongy roots that can grow in flooded soil.

Plants also need to reproduce. A plant that lives long enough to make seeds passes its helpful traits on. Over time, the plants with the best traits for a place are the ones that keep growing there.

🎬 Director's cut

The fern that comes back to life

This fern grows on the branches of live oak trees all over Florida. In a dry spell, it curls up and turns brown. It looks dead!

Scientists say it can lose almost all of its water and still live. After a good rain, it can turn green again in about a day. It even flew into space on the space shuttle in 1997.

⭐ Key idea: Plants have traits that help them survive in their environment. A trait that gives a plant an advantage is called an adaptation, and a trait that helps in one place may not help at all in another.
