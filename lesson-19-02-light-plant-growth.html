☀️ Part 1Why Plants Need Light

Plants make their own food using sunlight. This is called photosynthesis. Without enough light, a plant can't make the energy it needs to grow tall and strong.

When a plant gets plenty of light, it grows faster. When light is weak or blocked, growth slows down, sometimes a lot.

🔬 Part 2A Fair Test Changes One Thing

In a fair test, scientists change only one thing at a time. In this investigation, the only difference between the two plants is light. The soil, the water, and the pot all stay the same.

That way, if the plants grow differently, we know light is the reason. The one thing we change on purpose is called the variable.

🎬 Behind the scenes · Taller isn't always healthier
This plant grew in a dark basement. It stretched up tall, reaching for light. But its leaves are yellow and its stem is thin and weak. A plant in the dark can grow tall for a while, using food stored in its seed. When that food runs out, it stops growing. Taller isn't always healthier!

📏 Part 3Reading the Data

Scientists measure plant height in centimeters and write it in a data table. Then they build a graph, so the pattern is easy to see.

Light changes things in nature, too. Sunlight gets dimmer as it goes down through water. Let's ride down and find out where plants can still grow.

🎬 Director's cut

A garden with 180 lamps

There's no sunshine inside the space station, so NASA built plant chambers full of lamps. One of them, the Advanced Plant Habitat, has about 180 small lamps that scientists can change from Earth.

They turn some colors up and others down, one change at a time, to see which light helps plants grow best. It's a fair test, in space!

⭐ Key idea: Light is a growing condition for plants. The more light a plant gets, the more food it can make, and the faster it grows. Comparing plants with only one difference gives us evidence.
