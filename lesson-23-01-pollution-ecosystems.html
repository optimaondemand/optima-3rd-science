🌱 Part 1What Is Pollution?

Pollution happens when harmful stuff ends up somewhere it doesn't belong. There are three main types.

Land pollution is trash and chemicals left on the ground. Water pollution is oil, plastic, or waste in rivers and oceans. Air pollution is smoke and gases that make the air unhealthy to breathe. Each type can damage the living things nearby.

🌤 Part 2How Does Pollution Hurt Living Things?

Every living thing needs clean water, clean air, and healthy soil to survive. When pollution gets into an ecosystem, it can make animals sick and destroy habitats.

Fish die in rivers full of chemicals. Birds get tangled in floating plastic. Trees weaken when acid from smoky air soaks into the soil. Even small amounts of pollution can cause big problems over time.

🎬 Behind the scenes · Hungry manatees
Manatees eat seagrass. In 2021, algae blooms in Florida's Indian River Lagoon blocked the sunlight, and much of the seagrass there died. Many manatees went hungry. Scientists say cutting the pollution that feeds algae is the way to bring the seagrass back. (Photo: a manatee at Crystal River, Florida.)

🌍 Part 3Why Do Ecosystems Need Our Help?

Humans cause most pollution, from factories, cars, farming, and trash. But humans can fix it, too. Laws set limits on how much pollution factories can release. More than 400 national park sites protect wild places. Scientists clean up oil spills, plant trees, and restore rivers.

In the Chesapeake Bay, decades of teamwork by scientists, governments, and everyday people cut water pollution and helped bring back striped bass. The work is still going.

🎬 Director's cut

Parks that watch the air

The United States has more than 400 national park sites, and scientists check the air in many of them every day. At stations like Look Rock in Great Smoky Mountains National Park, machines measure smoke, haze, and acid in the rain.

After new clean-air laws in 1990, power plants and cars put out much less of the gases that make haze and acid rain. Mountains in the Smokies that haze used to hide are now easier to see.

⭐ Key idea: Pollution harms ecosystems by making the air, water, and land unsafe for living things. When the environment is polluted, plants and animals can't get what they need to survive. A single pollution source can affect many parts of an ecosystem at once, but people can take action to reduce the harm.
