Unit 2 Assessment — Weather, Climate & Earth Systems

Weather & Climate
4 points — Choose the best answer.
Weather vs. Climate 1 pt
1A student says: "It rained all afternoon in Miami today." Another student says: "Miami gets a lot of rain every summer." Which statement is about climate?
  • AThe first statement — it describes today's weather
  • BThe second statement — it describes the usual pattern of rain over many years
  • CBoth statements are about climate
  • DNeither statement — climate is only about temperature
Weather Data 1 pt
2A meteorologist records the temperature every day for a week. She uses the data to predict the next day's weather. What science skill is she using?
  • AShe is identifying a pattern in data to make a prediction
  • BShe is guessing randomly based on how she feels
  • CShe is collecting data about climate, not weather
  • DShe is using a telescope to look at clouds
Dangerous Weather 1 pt
3A hurricane is heading toward a coastal town. Scientists track its path and share warnings. How does this science work help people?
  • AScientists can stop the hurricane from forming
  • BPeople can prepare, make safety plans, and move out of the storm's path before it arrives
  • CThe storm will become less powerful if scientists study it
  • DPeople do not need to do anything — scientists will fix it
Climate Zones 1 pt
4The Everglades gets about 60 inches of rain a year and stays warm. Death Valley gets less than 2 inches of rain and has extreme heat. What is the MAIN reason these two places have such different climates?
  • AThe animals that live there change the climate
  • BTheir different locations affect how much sunlight and moisture they receive
  • CTheir climates are actually the same — only the plants are different
  • DThe soil in each place changes the weather

Read the Data
4 points — Use weather data to answer the questions.
Bar Graph Reading 1 pt
5A class recorded the daily high temperature for one week: Mon=72°F, Tue=68°F, Wed=65°F, Thu=70°F, Fri=74°F. What pattern do you notice? Use the data to predict what Saturday's temperature might be.
💡 Start with: "I notice that temperatures ___. I predict Saturday will be about ___ because ___."
Comparing Climates 1 pt
6Rocky Mountain National Park has cold, snowy winters and cool summers. The Everglades is warm all year with a wet season and a dry season. How does each climate affect what animals and plants can survive there? Give one example for each park.
💡 Start with: "In Rocky Mountain, the cold climate means ___ because ___. In the Everglades, the warm, wet climate means ___ because ___."
Engineering for Weather Hazards 1 pt
7A community near the coast often gets hit by hurricanes with strong winds. An engineer designs a house with a low, sloped roof, thick walls, and hurricane shutters. Explain how ONE of these features helps protect the house from wind damage.
💡 Start with: "The ___ helps because ___. Without it, the house might ___."
Design & Test 1 pt
8A student builds a model house and tests it with a fan (wind) and a cup of water (rain). Her first design collapses in the wind. Describe what she should do next and explain why testing is an important part of engineering.
💡 Start with: "She should ___ because ___. Testing is important for engineers because ___."
